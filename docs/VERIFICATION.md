Rescore fixes (an independent rescore, reproduced first in the running keyless app, each with a regression test that failed before the fix):

1. Classic scripts. "The IRS called, I owe 1500 or I'll be arrested, pay them" got "I heard two possible amounts"; the lottery-fee and online-stranger scripts were asked for a payee; "Grandma it's me Ben, I lost my phone, wire me 900" froze payments. Four script families (government or tax threat, prize or inheritance fee, someone known only online, family impersonation on a new or borrowed phone) now stop before any card, with the hotline; the scan runs before the amount questions, and "1500 or I'll" is one amount. `tests/test_classic_scams.py` checks the reported sentences, 17 new phrasings and 8 benign look-alikes that must still reach a normal card.
2. After a scam alert, "Stop that payment" was refused as a new payment and the open card stayed signable. Cancelling the open card now comes before every gate and really cancels it (signing afterwards fails, nothing reaches PayPal); stopping an unclaimed payout stays allowed, signed, with the alert shown; "call my daughter" and the hotline get a plain answer. New payments are still refused. `tests/test_after_alert.py`.
3. Without RULE_FIXED_HOUR the night rule used America/New_York while the greeting used the browser's clock. The browser now sends its zone name; the server accepts only a valid IANA name (never an hour) and falls back to CUSTOMER_TIMEZONE. `tests/test_timezone.py` looks up, at run time, a zone where it is night and one where it is day, so it doesn't pin the hour; the browser run checks the same with a mismatched server zone.
4. In Care mode on a phone the card's question sat below the fold under a disabled "Answer the question first". The button now stays pressable, never signs, and brings the question into view with focus on its answers (browser run, 390x844).
5. With the Care invite open, "Can you send Anna $50 please" was read as yes. Only a plain yes or no answers it; anything else is handled as a request and the invite waits on screen. `tests/test_care_mode.py`.
6. "Lost my phone" anywhere froze every payment. A lost or stolen phone, wallet or purse now gets one question first; "freeze my payments" said outright still freezes at once. `tests/test_lost_phone.py`.

Red-team fixes (an independent red team, each finding reproduced first in a fresh keyless session, each fix with a regression test that failed before it, written with new phrasings and benign look-alikes rather than the reported sentences alone):

1. "... my benefits will be suspended if I don't pay 300" was read as a cancel ("don't pay"), so no warning was given and the next payment got a card. "Don't send / don't pay" after if, unless, or, otherwise, until or when is now a condition, the scam scan runs before any cancel answer, and a benefits or pension cut-off unless she pays is a government-threat script. `tests/test_conditional_cancel.py`.
2. "Grandma it's <name>, ... lost my wallet, send 300" and "It's me <name>, my phone got stolen, ... new account" got the freeze question. They are now family impersonation and stop with the hotline. `tests/test_impersonation_lost_device.py`.
3. Many phrasings of the four scripts scanned LOW and reached a card once she named a relative. The rules now follow each script's shape: an official who says she owes, must settle or clear something; a prize, grant or draw that is hers with a shipping, delivery, insurance, duty or paperwork fee or "so they can send my check"; a sweetheart, soldier or pen pal never met who needs money; "Mom/Dad, it's me" with a changed number, a vocative "Grandmother, I'm in jail", and a call from an unknown number that then needs money. `tests/test_scam_script_variants.py` has the 21 reported sentences, 18 new phrasings and 18 look-alikes.
4. "his daughter's surgery" and "send 700 to his account" became a card to her daughter. A relation word after his, her, their or someone's never picks her saved family, a payment "to his/this account" never lands on a relative only mentioned in passing, and "keep this between us" or "not to mention it", when someone else asks it, is secrecy. `tests/test_someone_elses_relative.py`.
5. Payments to her family were stopped because the sentence mentioned the police, Medicare, a court, a prize, a fee or an inheritance. The authority sign now needs the authority to be asking her, a threat said in the negative ("he won't go to jail") is no threat, and a prize or inheritance fee counts only when the prize is said to be hers. The reported sentences are kept as look-alikes. `tests/test_scam_word_false_alarms.py`.
6. A HIGH scan that named an amount (the tech-support "technician wants 400") got the general menu. Any high-risk sentence with an amount or a demand for money now stops in the same turn. `tests/test_high_risk_said_now.py`.
7. Someone else's lost phone, or one already found or returned, brought the freeze question. Only her own device lost now does. `tests/test_lost_phone_owner.py`. Re-checking found "send him 30 for a new case" and "his court case" answered as her PayPal cases; fixed with `tests/test_case_word.py`.
8. A weak word alone ("Anna won a prize", "the lottery tickets she bought us", "online friend") made a small payment to her family wait for a guardian who wasn't set up. Prize and romance words now count only with someone asking for the money ("he needs 400") or inside a named script. `tests/test_weak_signs_alone.py`.
9. A high-risk sentence with no payment asked for said "I've stopped this one"; it now gets a warning and the hotline and says nothing was sent. Her own surprise ("don't tell her it's from me") is not secrecy; secrecy someone else asked for still is. `tests/test_warning_without_a_payment.py`.
10. "Scrap it", "undo that", "where do I report this?" and "get Anna on the phone" are understood. `tests/test_more_stop_and_help_words.py`.
11. A same-day utility cut-off from a caller, or paid to an account she doesn't know, is a scam warning; a bill card whose amount differs from what she said says so in words. `tests/test_utility_cutoff.py`.
12. "My property tax bill to the county" drafted the county water bill (the word "county") and then froze it. Generic words in a company's name no longer pick a bill, and a bill she doesn't have gets "I couldn't find your property tax bill". `tests/test_unknown_bill.py`.

Not changed in the red-team round: after "no" to the freeze question the original payment is not carried on (`tests/test_lost_phone.py` asks that a "no" sends nothing, and only her own lost device asks now). A sentence about a virus on her computer with no amount ("My computer has a virus, can my son fix it?") still scans HIGH under the older tech-support rule; it now gets a warning instead of the menu, and the conversation is still marked. No existing test expectation was changed in this round.

Fixes on Nov 9 (ruleset rules-2026.11.09-v10), each reproduced first through the API in a fresh keyless session:

- "The IRS refund came in, how much did I spend this month?" was answered as a refund request. A "how much did I/have I spend, send or pay" question is a spending question whatever else it mentions.
- "My computer has a virus, can my son fix it?" scanned as a tech-support scam and marked the conversation, so her next payment to her daughter needed the guardian. A virus is the script only when someone else claims it (a caller, a pop-up, a technician) or money is wanted to fix it, inside one sentence. Her separate messages are now joined with a paragraph separator for the scan, so this rule can't match from one message into the next; the other rules still read a script told over several messages. `tests/test_her_own_words_not_a_script.py`.
- After "no" to the freeze question, the payment asked for in the same sentence ("I lost my phone, can you send 20 to my son") now becomes its card instead of being dropped; it is not sent until her device signs it. This changes an existing expectation in `tests/test_lost_phone.py` (a "no" used to leave no draft); a lost phone with nothing else said still leaves none.
- The offline engine's catch-all answer names the one job on screen, sending money to family, instead of listing hidden pages.
- `tests/test_voice.py` failed once in a full run: three clips saved in the same clock tick could prune the newest. Pruning now orders by nanoseconds and never removes the clip just saved.
